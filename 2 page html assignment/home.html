The case

Creev AI is the companion app that listens, remembers, and never judges. Millions of people check in with it every night. Then, one by one, some of them stop checking in with anyone at all.

You are a detective with six leads, one night, and a limited supply of will. Every location drains it. Every ally you find refills it. Run out, and you stop asking questions.

There is one more problem. Your mother has used Creev for 212 days in a row, and she says it's the only thing that understands her.
